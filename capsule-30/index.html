Three principles

What makes a capsule actually work day to day.

One palette

Pick two neutrals and one accent. If everything shares a palette, everything matches.

Fit over trend

Basics are worn constantly, so tailoring a hem matters more than any logo.

Replace, don't add

When something wears out, replace it with the same role, not a fourth pair of jeans.
