Three setup rules

Get these right and every piece of gear works better.

Top of screen at eye level

Your eyes should land on the top third of the display without tilting your head.

Elbows at 90°

Keyboard low enough that your shoulders stay relaxed.

Light from the side

Put the main light beside the screen or above it, never behind it.
