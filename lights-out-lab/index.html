Three free habits

The gear helps more when these are in place.

Same wake time

Even on weekends. It anchors your body clock more than bedtime does.

Morning light

Ten minutes outside soon after waking helps you feel sleepy at the right time that night.

Screens down early

Swap the phone for a book or a podcast in the last half hour.
