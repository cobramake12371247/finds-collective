Three free upgrades

Do these before buying anything.

Turn on game mode

Most TVs have a game mode that cuts input lag a lot. It's in the picture settings.

Wire what you can

An ethernet cable to the console beats Wi-Fi for steady online play.

Screen at eye level

Tilt the top of the monitor toward you and keep it about an arm's length away.
