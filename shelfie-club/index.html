Three ground rules

Simple habits that make any routine work better.

Patch test

Try every new product on a small spot of your jaw for two days before using it all over.

One at a time

Add new products one by one, two weeks apart, so you know what's working.

Give it 6 weeks

Skin renews in cycles. Most products need about six weeks before you judge them.
